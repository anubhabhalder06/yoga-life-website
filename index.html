About the Studio

Where Practice Meets Purpose

Yoga Life is Baruipur's most trusted yoga & Zumba studio. Under the guidance of Mrs. Runu Halder Das — a qualified trainer with over a decade of experience — more than 500 students have discovered strength, stillness, and community here.

We offer both offline (in-studio) and online live classes, designed for every age and level — from beginners finding their first breath to seasoned practitioners deepening their practice.
